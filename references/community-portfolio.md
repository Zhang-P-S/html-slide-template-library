# Community Portfolio

Use this family when the deck should behave like a catalog, atlas, fandom showcase, gallery, or browseable collection.

## Default Direction

`atlas-card`

## Directions

### atlas-card

- Best for: pokedex, member atlas, knowledge card, item catalog, structured collection display
- What it looks like: light base, clearly separated cards, label systems, short descriptors, and easy scanning
- Cover syntax: catalog or archive-style identity page
- Section syntax: visible tag or category switching
- Content grammar: grid cards, labels, short descriptors, thumbnail-led browsing
- Preserve: browseability, indexing logic, label clarity
- Avoid: turning it into a pitch deck or a magazine spread when the content is really categorical

### magazine-album

- Best for: portfolio, curation, travel, lifestyle, visual collections, image-led storytelling
- What it looks like: warm neutrals, larger image moments, more editorial pacing, more breathing room than atlas-card
- Cover syntax: large image plus generous composition
- Section syntax: magazine-spread rhythm
- Content grammar: large image with short copy, alternating spreads, album pacing
- Preserve: visual rhythm, curation, image-first pacing
- Avoid: forcing dense catalog data into pages meant for slower viewing

### event-memory

- Best for: club recap, annual memory book, travel plan recap, social retrospective
- What it looks like: more playful and social than the other two directions, with timeline or activity rhythm
- Cover syntax: event image plus event name
- Section syntax: visible timeline or stage nodes
- Content grammar: photo wall, people pages, recap page, timeline page
- Preserve: memory and social warmth
- Avoid: making it too formal or too institutional unless the event itself requires it
