# Template Families

This file defines the six stable template families and their subtemplate directions.

## 1. academic-defense

### blue-white-evidence

- Best for: scholarship defense, honor review, comprehensive quality showcase
- Palette: light blue, white, dark blue, with restrained red or gold accents
- Cover syntax: identity block, clear title, stable footer bar, optional campus or documentary image
- Section syntax: numbered cards, four-part or five-part agenda
- Content grammar: certificate walls, screenshot walls, KPI table plus proof materials
- Tone: evidence-first, formal, clear

### deep-blue-documentary

- Best for: growth milestones, stage summary, solemn personal retrospective
- Palette: deep navy, near-black, white, restrained gold or red accents
- Cover syntax: night scene, architecture, silhouette, documentary framing
- Section syntax: large-number chapter divider with atmospheric background
- Content grammar: milestone timeline, honor collage, one-page strong claim
- Tone: documentary, solemn, closing-weight

### white-paper-defense

- Best for: thesis briefing, proposal defense, midterm research review
- Palette: white, light gray, blue-gray
- Cover syntax: report title block, optional bilingual subtitle, date or affiliation area
- Section syntax: report-like numbered sections
- Content grammar: method slide, experiment slide, result charts, conclusion slide
- Tone: rational, research-facing, restrained

## 2. policy-and-governance

### red-gold-outline

- Best for: policy outline, plan interpretation, task briefing
- Palette: red, gold, white, light warm neutrals
- Cover syntax: centered or near-centered heavyweight title, ceremonial framing
- Section syntax: "Part X" plus title, visible red tags or capsules
- Content grammar: task checklist, key numbers, timeline, policy explanation blocks
- Tone: official, authoritative, structured

### party-history-documentary

- Best for: themed education, party-history narrative, red-culture retrospectives
- Palette: deep red, bright red, white, gold
- Cover syntax: ribbon, skyline, memorial architecture, narrative atmosphere
- Section syntax: title-led divider with image atmosphere
- Content grammar: event timeline, image documentary, spirit summary, retrospection
- Tone: commemorative, narrative, emotionally weighted

### blue-red-briefing

- Best for: digital governance, industrial policy, modern state-capacity themes
- Palette: navy, red, white, light gray
- Cover syntax: modern sans-serif briefing look instead of ceremonial poster style
- Section syntax: horizontal briefing dividers
- Content grammar: framework diagrams, indicator summary, project examples, governance modules
- Tone: policy plus modern industry expression

## 3. engineering-and-technology

### light-blue-system

- Best for: system design, technical explanation, engineering principle walkthrough
- Palette: light blue, white, dark blue
- Cover syntax: product render or configuration image plus stable title block
- Section syntax: module numbering and clear function zoning
- Content grammar: architecture diagram, module breakdown, process flow, parameter tables
- Tone: structured, technical, rational

### deep-space-configuration

- Best for: aerospace, drones, robotics, payload, mission architecture
- Palette: deep blue, black, bright blue
- Cover syntax: strong hero image on dark background
- Section syntax: large number plus mission image
- Content grammar: configuration view, mission scene, performance highlights, task flow
- Tone: technical, high-scene, visually stronger

### white-lab-results

- Best for: experiment validation, performance testing, algorithm evaluation
- Palette: white, gray, blue
- Cover syntax: concise title with minimal instrumentation
- Section syntax: experiment-stage-oriented divider
- Content grammar: setup photo, curves, comparison tables, error analysis
- Tone: lab-oriented, results-first

## 4. theory-and-coursework

### black-white-argument

- Best for: theory discussion, classroom presentation, philosophy and social-science argument
- Palette: black, white, gray
- Cover syntax: large title, low decoration, argument-first
- Section syntax: number plus thesis-like section sentence
- Content grammar: concept contrast, logical progression, quote boxes
- Tone: argumentative, direct, concept-led

### paper-quotation

- Best for: close reading, classic text interpretation, lecture-style coursework
- Palette: warm paper white, charcoal, restrained soft accents
- Cover syntax: printed-page or lecture-note mood
- Section syntax: like a handout or annotated reading page
- Content grammar: long quotation, marginal note, paragraph breakdown, author comparison
- Tone: reading-oriented, textual, editorial

### cool-gray-concept

- Best for: tech ethics, social impact, interdisciplinary concept reports
- Palette: cool gray, white, blue-gray
- Cover syntax: quiet modern concept framing
- Section syntax: concept-led chapter title
- Content grammar: relation maps, issue decomposition, conclusion lists
- Tone: abstract, calm, contemporary

## 5. innovation-roadshow

### tech-launch

- Best for: product launch, competition pitch, project release
- Palette: blue, white, controlled bright accents
- Cover syntax: strong title plus project hero visual
- Section syntax: pain point, solution, highlights, outcome, future
- Content grammar: feature cards, solution map, result board, prospect slide
- Tone: launch-like, forward-moving, polished

### outcome-storyline

- Best for: project summary, team showcase, annual innovation narrative
- Palette: light base, dark title, restrained emphasis colors
- Cover syntax: identity plus journey framing
- Section syntax: phase transition with clear progress rhythm
- Content grammar: project timeline, collaboration, stage outcomes, applied value
- Tone: narrative, outcome-driven

### black-gold-impact

- Best for: finals, stage demo, climax reveal, high-pressure showcase
- Palette: black, gold, small amount of bright emphasis
- Cover syntax: oversized type, high-contrast hero image
- Section syntax: low-density, high-rhythm section dividers
- Content grammar: big-number page, strong-claim page, visual impact page
- Tone: high-pressure, commanding, dramatic

## 6. community-portfolio

### atlas-card

- Best for: pokedex, member atlas, knowledge card, item catalog
- Palette: white or light base with multiple tag colors
- Cover syntax: catalog or archive-style identity page
- Section syntax: visible tag or category switching
- Content grammar: grid cards, labels, short descriptors, thumbnail-led browsing
- Tone: indexed, browsable, encyclopedia-like

### magazine-album

- Best for: portfolio, curation, travel, lifestyle, visual collections
- Palette: warm neutrals with soft contrast colors
- Cover syntax: large image plus generous composition
- Section syntax: magazine-spread rhythm
- Content grammar: large image with short copy, alternating spreads, album pacing
- Tone: aesthetic, editorial, image-led

### event-memory

- Best for: club event recap, annual memory book, travel plan, community retrospective
- Palette: theme-tuned and more playful than the other families
- Cover syntax: event image plus event name
- Section syntax: visible timeline or stage nodes
- Content grammar: photo wall,人物页, recap page, timeline page
- Tone: memory-driven, social, light narrative
