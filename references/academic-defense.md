# Academic Defense

Use this family when the deck needs to feel formal, evidence-first, and academically reliable.

## Default Direction

`blue-white-evidence`

## Directions

### blue-white-evidence

- Best for: scholarship defense, honor review, comprehensive quality showcase
- What it looks like: light blue and white base, dark blue framing, numbered agenda cards, screenshot walls, KPI or certificate-heavy evidence pages
- Cover syntax: identity block, main title, optional subtitle, stable footer bar, optional campus or documentary background
- Section syntax: four-part or five-part numbered agenda
- Content grammar: certificate walls, screenshot walls, KPI table plus proof materials, two-column evidence summaries
- Preserve: order, evidence density, stable header and footer framing
- Avoid: flashy gradients, startup-launch chrome, decorative stock imagery

### deep-blue-documentary

- Best for: milestones, stage summary, solemn academic or personal retrospective
- What it looks like: deep navy or near-black backgrounds, stronger atmosphere, milestone progression, more restrained text, stronger closing tone
- Cover syntax: night scene, architecture, silhouette, documentary framing
- Section syntax: large-number chapter divider with atmosphere
- Content grammar: timeline, honor collage, one-page strong claim, milestone recap
- Preserve: documentary seriousness, pacing, visual restraint
- Avoid: overloading every slide with dense small text

### white-paper-defense

- Best for: thesis briefing, proposal defense, midterm review, method-and-result reporting
- What it looks like: white or very light base, chart-first pages, research-report rhythm, cleaner and more analytical than the evidence-heavy direction
- Cover syntax: report title block, optional bilingual subtitle, date or affiliation area
- Section syntax: report-like numbered sections
- Content grammar: method page, result page, chart page, conclusion page
- Preserve: rationality, chart space, academic calm
- Avoid: certificate-wall grammar when the deck is really paper-oriented
